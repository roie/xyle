About Riverbend

The crew behind Riverbend

Riverbend Plumbing started in 1998 out of one van and a toolbox.

We are a family-owned company.

Based in
Edmonton, Alberta
On call
Every day, around the clock

01 / Our start

Built one careful repair at a time.

The first Riverbend jobs came through neighbours and word of mouth. That is still how much of our work finds us today.

We keep appointments realistic, explain what failed, and price the repair before the tools come out. If a smaller fix will do, we say so.

Do the small jobs well and the big jobs follow.

Riverbend shop rule, since 1998

02 / The shop

Experienced hands. Clear explanations.

Our licensed journeymen work alongside apprentices who are learning the trade the right way: safely, patiently, and without shortcuts.

  • Show up prepared.Stocked vans and a plan for the job.
  • Protect the home.Clean boots, drop cloths, tidy finishes.
  • Explain the work.Options in plain language before repairs begin.
